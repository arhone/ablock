# ABlock - Ad Blocker & Site Checker

Название и краткое описание берутся из `src/_locales/en/messages.json` (`extName`, `extDescription`), в консоли не вводятся.
В консоль вставляется текст из блока «Описание»; скриншоты — `../img/en/`.

**Название:** ABlock - Ad Blocker & Site Checker

**Краткое описание:** Blocks ads and trackers so pages load faster and use less data. Checks any site's speed, security and privacy.

## Описание

```text
ABlock blocks ads and trackers and shows, in one click, how fast, safe and private the site you are on is.

FASTER AND LIGHTER
Without ad scripts and trackers, pages load faster and use less mobile data and battery. Blocking is done by the browser itself, so the extension doesn't slow anything down.

SAFER
• Blocks known scam, phishing and malware sites.
• Hides cookie consent pop-ups.
• Regional filter lists turn on automatically for your languages.

SITE CHECK
• Score out of 10: speed, page weight, security and privacy.
• Who collects data on the page and what was blocked.
• HTTPS, security headers, technologies and hosting.
• Share the report as an image or text.

PRIVACY
Everything runs in your browser. No analytics, no accounts, no servers of our own.

If a site breaks, turn protection off for that site only.

Open source: https://github.com/arhone/ablock
Feedback: https://github.com/arhone/ablock/issues
Support the project: https://boosty.to/a.arh/donate
```
